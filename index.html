Ball Battle

Wall Multiplier

Every wall hit makes a ball faster and hit harder. Pick your fighters, give them powers, and watch the chaos. Last ball standing wins.

How to Play

Rules

  • Balls bounce around the arena on their own.
  • Each wall hit multiplies a ball's damage and boosts its speed.
  • When balls collide, each one deals its damage to the other.
  • The ring around a ball is its HP. Last ball alive wins.
  • Before starting, edit each ball's name, HP, damage and power.
